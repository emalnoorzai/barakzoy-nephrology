Patient & Family Guide

Preparing for Your Visit & Insurance

Simple, helpful details so your visit goes smoothly from start to finish.

What to Bring to Your Appointment

  • Photo ID & Insurance Cards: Please bring your driver's license/ID and primary and secondary insurance cards.
  • Medication Bottles or List: Bring all prescription bottles, over-the-counter medicines, and vitamins you take.
  • Recent Lab Results: Recent blood work (creatinine/eGFR) or kidney ultrasound reports if done outside our office.
  • Arrive 15 Minutes Early: Gives our friendly front desk time to complete your check-in comfortably.

Referrals & 24-Hour Cancellation Policy

  • Do You Need a Referral? Traditional Medicare and most PPO plans do not require a referral. HMO plans and Tricare Prime require a referral from your primary care doctor faxed to (904) 375-8568.
  • Appointment Length: First consultations take 30–45 minutes so Dr. Barakzoy never has to rush; follow-ups take 15–20 minutes.
  • 24-Hour Cancellation Notice: Please call (904) 579-3578 at least 24 hours ahead to reschedule. Missed visits without 24 hours notice carry a $25 fee (established patients) or $100 fee (new patients).

Accepted Health Insurance Plans

  • Medicare & Medicare Advantage: Traditional Medicare Part B, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, WellCare & Devoted Health.
  • Medicaid Plans: Florida Medicaid and participating Medicaid Managed Care plans.
  • Commercial & Military Plans: Florida Blue (BCBS), Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, UMR, Ambetter, AvMed & Tricare.

Billing & Good Faith Estimate

  • Payment Options: Copayments and deductibles are due at check-in. We accept major credit/debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, checks, and cash.
  • Self-Pay & Good Faith Estimate: Uninsured or self-pay patients receive discounted time-of-service rates and a written Good Faith Estimate before care under the federal No Surprises Act.
  • Billing Questions: Call (904) 579-3578 (Monday – Thursday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
Is Dr. Barakzoy accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients for kidney consultations, high blood pressure care, and second opinions at our Orange Park clinic.

What does a low eGFR or high creatinine mean?

eGFR measures how well your kidneys filter waste from your blood. Dr. Barakzoy reviews your eGFR trend, urine protein, and blood pressure together to create a plan that protects your remaining kidney function.

How do I request prescription refills or medical records?

For refills, ask your pharmacy to send an electronic request to our office or message us via the eCW Patient Portal. For medical records, complete our Records Release form below and fax it to (904) 375-8568.

Information for Referring Physicians & Care Facilities

Senior living communities and referring doctors may fax patient demographics, insurance, recent CMP/CBC/urinalysis labs, and referral notes to (904) 375-8568 or call (904) 579-3578 for urgent consultations.
