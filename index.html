Clinical Care & Specialties

Comprehensive Nephrology & Medical Services

When you are facing an unexpected kidney illness, uncontrolled hypertension, or electrolyte imbalance, our board-certified physician, nurses, and clinical staff deliver coordinated care across our clinic, hospitals, and dialysis centers.

🫘

Nephrology Services

Evidence-based management for Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) caused by hypertension, diabetes, lupus, or polycystic kidney disease—focused on slowing progression and protecting long-term kidney function.

🫀

Hypertension & Internal Medicine

Specialized evaluation and treatment for complex or resistant high blood pressure, cardiovascular risk reduction, and comprehensive adult internal medicine consultations.

🏥

Dialysis Care & Admissions

Ongoing physician oversight at Fresenius and DaVita Dialysis Centers throughout Northeast Florida. Our office coordinates directly with central admissions to place patients near their home.

🔬

Diagnostic Procedures & Imaging

Kidney patients frequently require renal ultrasounds and specialized diagnostic testing. Our clinical staff arranges appointments at convenient imaging facilities tailored to your location.

💊

Pharmacy & Medication Guidance

Because insurance formularies vary, our clinical team reviews your medication essentials and discusses effective covered alternatives. Please contact your pharmacy directly for routine refills.

🥗

Nutrition & Preventive Lifestyle

Managing sodium intake, healthy weight, blood sugar, and daily low-impact walking directly supports healthy blood pressure, improves circulation, and reduces strain on your kidneys.

Insurance Participation & Billing

Medical services are provided and billed directly by Ahmad S. Barakzoy, M.D., LLC. Dr. Barakzoy participates in most major health insurance plans, including Medicare and commercial carriers.

  • Please bring your current insurance card and photo ID to every visit.
  • Co-payments and deductibles are collected at the time of service.
  • Because every insurance plan has unique coverage rules, please contact your insurance provider directly regarding specific plan deductibles, referrals, or co-payment amounts.
